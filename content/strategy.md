# Piano Content Strategy

Oct 19, 2026 · Seth

## Goal & guiding principles

**Goal:** be the account people follow because they learn something, enjoy it, and come away trusting you. Sales should follow from that reputation, not from the posts asking for it.

Three words to test every video against: **knowledgeable, caring, entertaining.** Each video should clearly hit at least two of them.

- **Teach, don't pitch.** Fewer than 1 in 10 posts should mention a product, price or event, and those should still be useful on their own.
- **The showroom is the set, not the subject.** Pianos appear on camera all the time; they just aren't the call to action.
- **End on a question, not a link.** "Which one did you learn on?" builds an audience faster than "visit us."
- **Be fair to everyone.** Talk about all brands, players and budgets respectfully. Being honest about tradeoffs is what earns trust.
- **Make series, not one-offs.** Recurring formats are easier to batch-film and give people a reason to follow.

## Content mix

Recommended starting mix (share of posts) · revisit after 8 weeks:

| Pillar | Share of posts |
| --- | --- |
| Buying & ownership | 30% |
| How pianos work | 25% |
| Fun & challenges | 20% |
| People & community | 15% |
| History & heritage | 10% |

Lead with the two pillars that make people save and share (buying tips and how pianos work), since those build the "knowledgeable" reputation fastest. Fun and people content keeps the feed warm and human. History is the occasional treat.

| Pillar | What it covers | Builds | Already scripted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buying & ownership | Used-piano checks, brand myths, care, humidity, tuning, moving | Knowledgeable, caring | Used piano; Piano or the name; Still a Steinway? |
| How pianos work | What's inside, why it sounds the way it does, analogies like the brush video | Knowledgeable, entertaining | Paint with every brush; Does size matter? |
| Fun & challenges | Blind listening tests, guess-the-piano, showroom moments, quick playing clips | Entertaining | Strangest things inside a piano |
| People & community | Technicians, teachers, students, local performers, university partners, local businesses | Caring | Our new sign |
| History & heritage | How the piano was invented, famous instruments, how pianos are built | Knowledgeable, entertaining | — |

"Other" categories fold into these: a holiday piano tune goes in Fun, and a technician Q&A goes in People.

## Recurring series & idea bank

Give each pillar a named series with a consistent title card. Viewers learn what to expect, and you can film several episodes in one session.

**Buyer's Notebook** (Buying & ownership)

- 3 things to consider when buying a used piano ✓
- Are you buying the piano or the name? ✓
- Michigan winters and your piano: why humidity matters
- What a piano tuning actually is (and why once a year)
- Upright vs. grand: what really changes
- Moving a piano: why it's not a DIY job
- Questions to ask any seller, private or dealer
- Is it still a Steinway? (rebuilt pianos) ✓

**Under the Lid** (How pianos work)

- Paint with every brush ✓
- What happens in the half second after you press a key
- What the three pedals actually do
- Why a piano has around 230 strings, not 88
- What "voicing" means, and why two of the same model can sound different
- Slow-motion hammers
- Does size matter? ✓

**Blind Listen** (Fun & challenges)

- Guess the piano: same passage on three instruments, reveal at the end
- Digital or acoustic? Can you hear it through your phone speaker?
- New vs. 60-year-old piano, same chord
- "Play it on every piano in the showroom" (one tune, many instruments)
- Viewer requests: comment a song, you play it
- Guess the decade: same tune on pianos from the 1920s, 1960s and today
- Spot the out-of-tune note: the tech detunes one string, viewers guess, then the fix
- Blindfold touch test: acoustic vs. digital, or small vs. large grand, by feel
- Learn it in 60 seconds: a non-pianist coworker learns a melody
- One song, five styles: "Happy Birthday" as classical, ragtime, jazz, gospel and film score

**Shop Talk** (Fun & challenges)

- Strangest things found inside a piano ✓
- How do you get a 9-foot grand through a door? (moving time-lapse)
- All 88 keys in 30 seconds (slow-motion hammers, no talking)
- True or false, piano edition (quick-fire myths)

**Meet the…** (People & community)

- Meet the technician: a day of tuning and regulation
- A piano teacher's best advice for adult beginners
- A university student's practice routine
- Local performer spotlight
- First-piano stories, shared with the owner's permission

**Good Neighbors** (People & community)

- Our new sign: thank you, FastSigns and Light Bulb Co ✓

**Piano Past** (History & heritage)

- Who invented the piano, and why it was called "soft-loud"
- How the piano went from 5 octaves to 88 keys
- How a concert grand is built, in 60 seconds
- The story behind a famous piano or performance

## Cadence, platforms & measuring

**Cadence:** three short videos a week is sustainable and frequent enough to grow. Consistency matters more than volume. Two a week that you never miss beats five a week that stops in a month.

**Platforms:** film vertically once and post the same short to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, plus Facebook if your local customers are there. Longer cuts, like the full "Piano or the Name" draft, go on YouTube about once a month.

**Engagement is the job:** reply to every comment in the first hour or two after posting. For a reputation built on "caring," the replies matter as much as the videos.

**What to watch, by goal:**

| Signal | Tells you | Expect it from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saves | People found it useful enough to keep | Buyer's Notebook, Under the Lid |
| Shares | It's useful or fun enough to send to someone | Blind Listen, Under the Lid |
| Comments | You started a conversation | Blind Listen, question endings |
| Average watch time | The pacing and hook are working | Every video |
| Follows per video | It made people want more | Series episodes |

**Review after 8 weeks.** Look at which series earned the most saves, shares and comments. Shift the mix toward those, but keep every pillar in rotation so the page doesn't become one note.
