# Our New Sign — Script & Storyboard

Nov 17, 2026 · Seth

**Pillar:** People & community · **Series:** Good Neighbors (new: local partners and businesses) · **Launch slot:** Not yet scheduled

## Concept & format

A before-and-after story with a twist: the new pylon sign goes up, the lights come on, and something's wrong. Instead of ending there, two local businesses go out of their way to make it right, and the payoff is the sign lit up at night. It's a genuine thank-you, not an ad.

| Item | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Length | About 60 seconds (~140 words of VO) |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 vertical; the pylon is tall, so it suits vertical framing |
| Talent | You as narrator; Eric and Fred from FastSigns on camera if they agree |
| Setting | The pylon sign: old, during install, and finished at dusk |
| Goal | Show West Michigan Piano as a good neighbor that notices and credits good work; earn shares from FastSigns, Light Bulb Co and their followers |
| Tone | Warm and grateful, with a small "uh-oh" moment for story |

**Why it fits the strategy:** it hits "caring" directly. It also echoes a theme from the piano videos (what's inside matters as much as what's on the outside), which ties it back to who you are.

## Script

The story follows your account: old sign, new sign, bad ballasts, and two businesses that went the extra mile.

**0:00–0:05 — Hook**

ON SCREEN: OUR SIGN WAS FALLING APART.

VO: "Our old pylon sign was falling apart. So we called in the people who do all our signage."

**0:05–0:18 — The install**

ON SCREEN: THANK YOU, FASTSIGNS

VO: "FastSigns. They're friendly, and their work is always top quality. Eric and Fred came out to install the new signs and swap the light bulbs while they were up there."

**0:18–0:28 — The twist**

ON SCREEN: …UH-OH.

VO: "Then we flipped the power on, and it didn't look right. Eric and Fred helped us track it down: the ballasts were bad."

**0:28–0:42 — Going the extra mile**

ON SCREEN: SWITCHING TO LED

VO: "If we needed new ballasts anyway, we wanted to switch the whole sign to LED. That meant the specialty bulbs we'd just bought weren't needed. FastSigns didn't just shrug. They went to Light Bulb Co and asked if they'd take the bulbs back. And Light Bulb Co said yes."

**0:42–0:52 — The reveal**

ON SCREEN: FULLY UPDATED. FULLY LIT.

VO: "Now we've got a sign that matches our new look, fully updated inside and out."

**0:52–1:00 — Close**

ON SCREEN: THANK YOU, FASTSIGNS & LIGHT BULB CO

VO: "FastSigns' flexibility and Light Bulb Co's understanding really blew us away. That's what good neighbors look like. Who's a local business that went above and beyond for you? Give them a shout-out below."

**Notes on the script**

- **The "uh-oh" beat is the hook that keeps people watching.** A smooth install isn't a story; a problem that gets solved is.
- **The comment prompt turns it outward.** Asking viewers to shout out their own favorite local business makes the post about the community, not just about you, and it tends to get shared.
- **Optional piano tie-in:** after the reveal, add "Turns out a sign is like a piano: it's what's inside that counts." It links back to your other videos, but cut it if it feels forced.

## Before you post

**Permissions**

- [ ] Ask Eric and Fred whether they're comfortable appearing on camera and being named.
- [ ] Give FastSigns a heads-up and ask whether they'd like to be tagged. Most franchise locations will happily reshare.
- [ ] Ask Light Bulb Co the same, and get their correct name and social handle.

**Facts to confirm**

- [ ] That Light Bulb Co did take the bulbs back, so the video doesn't promise something that hasn't happened.
- [ ] The exact business names and spellings: the FastSigns location name and Light Bulb Co's full name.
- [ ] Whether the LED switch is finished. If not, film the reveal after it's done, or reword shot 9 to "almost there."

**Production notes**

- **Shoot the reveal at blue hour.** The 15–20 minutes after sunset make a lit sign glow against a deep blue sky instead of a black one.
- **Use what you have from install day.** Even shaky phone clips work here, because they feel real. Stills with a slow zoom fill any gaps.
- **Tag both businesses** in the caption and on-screen, and put the shout-out request in the caption too.
- **Caption everything;** most viewers watch muted.
- **Keep it a thank-you.** Don't mention pricing, and don't push "come visit us." The goodwill does the work.
